## 1. Core Concepts & Frameworks

### Affiliate Marketing Fundamentals
- **Affiliate marketing** is a performance-based model where a creator (publisher) earns a commission for driving sales/clicks to a merchant's product via a unique tracking link.
- **Key metrics**: CTR (click-through rate), CVR (conversion rate), EPC (earnings per click), commission rate, average order value (AOV)
- **Revenue formula**: Revenue = Traffic × CTR × CVR × AOV × Commission%

### Short-Form Video Affiliate Funnel
```
Awareness (hook) → Interest (problem framing) → Desire (demo + proof) → Action (CTA with link)
```
This AIDA funnel adapted for TikTok/Reels requires compression to 30–60 seconds. Every second must earn its place.

### Platform-Specific Models
| Platform | Affiliate Model | Best Content Length | Top Category 2025 |
|----------|----------------|--------------------|--------------------|
| TikTok Shop | TikTok Affiliate Program; in-app product links | 30–45s | Beauty, Electronics, Home Goods |
| Facebook/Instagram | Meta Affiliate Program; link in bio/stories | 15–30s Reels | Fashion, Fitness, Kitchen |
| Shopee Affiliate | Shopee Affiliate Link; external traffic | Any length | FMCG, Electronics, Fashion |
| Lazada Affiliate | Lazada Affiliate Program | Any length | Electronics, Home, Baby |

## 2. Hook Formulas (Proven Short-Form Video Hooks)

### The 5 Psychological Trigger Categories
1. **Curiosity Gap**: "You won't believe what this $12 gadget does to your..." → withholds resolution
2. **Pain Agitation**: "If you're still [doing X the hard way], you're wasting [time/money]..." → speaks to frustration
3. **Aspiration/Transformation**: "This is how I went from [before] to [after] in [timeframe]..." → social proof + desire
4. **Social Proof / Bandwagon**: "3 million people are obsessed with this product because..." → validates via others
5. **Shock/Surprise**: "I threw away [expensive thing] after trying this $X alternative..." → pattern interrupt

### Hook Effectiveness Scoring Rubric (1–10)
| Dimension | 1–3 (Weak) | 4–6 (Acceptable) | 7–10 (Strong) |
|-----------|-----------|------------------|--------------|
| Specificity | Generic claim | Category-specific | Product + outcome specific |
| Emotional charge | Neutral | Mild emotion | Strong emotional resonance |
| Audience relevance | Off-target | Broad fit | Direct pain-point match |
| Pattern interrupt | Common phrase | Moderately unusual | Genuinely surprising |

### Minimum passing score: 7/10 (weighted average across all 4 dimensions)

## 3. Content Structure Templates

### Standard 60-Second Affiliate Video Script Structure
```
[0–3s]  HOOK — one sentence that stops the scroll
[3–8s]  PROBLEM — name the pain point the audience knows
[8–25s] DEMO — show the product solving the problem (visual + narration)
[25–45s] PROOF — social proof: reviews, before/after, numbers ("50,000 sold")
[45–55s] CTA — single action: "Link in bio" / "Comment SEND for the link" / "Click the cart icon"
[55–60s] AFFILIATE DISCLOSURE — "This is an affiliate link, I earn a commission"
```

### Caption Template
```
[Emoji] [Product Name] changed my [relevant routine/life aspect]! 

[1 sentence on the main benefit]

[Affiliate disclosure: #ad or "This post contains affiliate links"]

[5–10 hashtags: mix of niche (#skincareproducts), trending (#tiktokmademebuyit), and branded]
```

## 6. Platform Algorithm Signals (2025)

### TikTok For You Page (FYP) Ranking Factors
1. **Completion rate** — most important; aim for > 70% video completion
2. **Engagement rate** — likes, comments, shares, saves within first hour
3. **Rewatch rate** — videos watched multiple times signal high value
4. **Click-through to product** — in-app product link clicks are a direct signal
5. **Comment keywords** — asking users to "comment SEND" creates engagement spike
6. **Hashtag relevance** — mix of niche + trending, max 3–5 hashtags

### Facebook/Instagram Reels Distribution Factors
1. **Watch time and completion rate** — completion > 80% triggers wider distribution
2. **Shares to Stories** — highest weight signal for reach
3. **Saves** — indicates long-term content value
4. **Non-follower reach** — reels are shown to non-followers; hook must work without context
5. **Posting time** — highest engagement 12–2pm and 7–9pm local time (audience-dependent)

## 7. Compliance & Legal Requirements

### FTC Affiliate Disclosure (US)
- Must clearly disclose the affiliate relationship **before the affiliate link** appears
- Acceptable disclosures: "#ad", "#sponsored", "Affiliate link", "I earn a commission"
- Must be prominent, not buried in hashtags
- Source: FTC Guides Concerning the Use of Endorsements and Testimonials (16 CFR Part 255)

### Platform-Specific Rules
| Platform | Key Rule | Prohibited Content |
|----------|---------|-------------------|
| TikTok | Must use TikTok's native affiliate link system for Shop links | Guaranteed income claims, medical claims without disclosure |
| Facebook/Meta | Must use Facebook's branded content tools when paid partnership exists | Financial advice, health claims, weapons |
| Shopee/Lazada | Affiliate links must be disclosed in caption | Counterfeit products, price manipulation |
